Our story

Barber opened its doors with a single chair and a simple promise: a great cut, every time. Word spread, the chairs multiplied, and today our team of experienced barbers serves the whole neighbourhood.

We still believe in the fundamentals — sharp tools, hot towels, honest advice and taking the time to get it right. Come see the difference for yourself.
